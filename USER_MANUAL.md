# RxDesk Pharmacy & Doctor Directory - User Manual

## 1. Overview
RxDesk provides an integrated platform for pharmacy billing and doctor discovery. This manual covers how to manage your pharmacy profile, doctor chambers, and local SEO discovery features.

---

## 2. Shop Registration
To start using RxDesk, you must register your shop.
1.  **Mobile Registration**: Use the RxDesk Mobile App to create your profile. This captures your high-accuracy GPS coordinates automatically.
2.  **Verification**: Your shop must be verified by an admin before it appears in public search results.
3.  **Role**: Once registered, your user role changes to `shop_owner`, giving you access to the Web Dashboard.

---

## 3. Web Dashboard Settings
Access your settings at `rxdesk.in/dashboard/settings` to manage your shop's digital identity.
*   **GST Registration**: Choose between *Unregistered*, *Composite*, or *Regular* GST types.
*   **Billing Settings**: Configure Thermal vs A4 printing, HSN visibility, and Batch number display.
*   **Opening Balances**: Set your initial Cash and Bank balances for accurate accounting.

---

## 4. Pharmacy SEO & Location Management
Your pharmacy's visibility on the "Near Pharmacy" search depends on your GPS coordinates.
*   **Mobile Setup (Recommended)**: Registering or updating your profile via the mobile app automatically captures high-accuracy GPS coordinates.
*   **Web Management**: You can verify or fine-tune your location from the **Web Dashboard > Settings**.
*   **Manual Overwrite**: If your mobile GPS was inaccurate, you can manually enter Latitude and Longitude from a Google Maps pin into the Web Settings.
*   **Sync Button**: Click "Sync My Location" on your laptop (if you are at the shop) to update coordinates instantly.

---

## 5. View Your Public Profile
Every pharmacy on RxDesk gets a professional, SEO-optimized public landing page.
*   **URL**: `https://rxdesk.in/pharmacy/[your-shop-id]`
*   **Direct Link**: Click the **View Public Profile** button at the top of your Shop Settings to see your live page.
*   **Sharing**: Share this URL on WhatsApp, Google My Business, or Social Media so patients can find your timings and available doctors.

---

## 6. Doctor Chamber Management
If your pharmacy hosts doctors, you can manage their schedules directly.
1.  **Add a Doctor**: Go to the **Chambers** tab and click **Add New Doctor**.
2.  **Set Timings**: Define weekly consultation hours (e.g., *Mon & Wed, 5 PM - 8 PM*).
3.  **Set Fees**: Display consultation fees for patient transparency.
4.  **Automatic Sync**: These details appear instantly on your public pharmacy profile.

---

## 7. Search & Discovery
Patients visit `rxdesk.in` to find healthcare nearby.
*   **Find Pharmacy**: Uses GPS (mobile) or Area search (desktop) to find shops within 10km.
*   **Find Doctor**: Allows searching by Name, Specialization (e.g., "ENT"), or Symptoms.
*   **Directions**: Patients can click "Directions" to navigate to your shop via Google Maps.

---

## 8. Accounting & Backup
*   **GSTR Reports**: Automatic generation of GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B data from your billing.
*   **Data Safety**: Download a full JSON backup of your accounting data at any time from the Settings page.
*   **Restore**: Use the "Upload & Restore" feature to migrate your data if you change computers.
